This Privacy Policy explains how the Aerospace Multidisciplinary Design Optimization Laboratory (AeroMDO Lab) at Istanbul Technical University (ITU) handles personal data collected through this website (aeromdo.itu.edu.tr). We collect as little data as possible and never sell it.

01Who We Are

This website is operated by AeroMDO Lab, Faculty of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Istanbul Technical University, Maslak, 34469 Istanbul, Türkiye. For the purposes of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), Istanbul Technical University acts as the data controller.

02Data We Collect

  • Newsletter: if you subscribe via the “Stay Connected” form, we store your email address.
  • Contact: if you email us or use the contact form, we receive your name, email address and the content of your message.
  • Technical data: like most websites, the university’s web server automatically records standard log data such as IP address, browser type, date and time of access and pages visited.

We do not ask for or knowingly collect sensitive personal data, and we do not create user accounts.

03How We Use Your Data

  • To send lab news, events and announcements you subscribed to.
  • To reply to your enquiries and collaboration requests.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

Your data is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

04Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Embedded or linked third-party content (for example fonts, maps or social media) may set their own cookies under their respective policies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

05Sharing and Third Parties

We share personal data only with ITU’s IT services that host this website, or where required by law. Links to external sites (publishers, LinkedIn, Instagram, X/Twitter, Google Scholar, ResearchGate) are governed by those sites’ own privacy policies.

06Data Retention

Newsletter email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Enquiry emails are kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up. Server logs are retained according to ITU’s IT policies.

07Your Rights

Under KVKK (Article 11) and, where applicable, the EU GDPR, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may object to its processing. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.

08Contact

For any privacy question or request, please contact us through the contact section of this website.

09Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
